Direct link to the MP4

  1. Part 1, camera. A physical iPhone 15 Pro and the NFC tag on a desk. The tag is paired through the system NFC sheet, apps are bricked, a blocked app shows the shield, and the tag is scanned again to unbrick.
  2. Part 2, screen recording. The same flow at readable size: pair a tag, create a preset, pick apps, brick, shield, scan to unbrick.

Pairing in the video uses "Pair another slab" in Settings. It is the same NFCTagReaderSession flow as onboarding step 04 on a fresh install. Reviewers without an NFC tag can enter the code APPREVIEW on that onboarding step to pair a virtual key.
