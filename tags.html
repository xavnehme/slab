Where to get a slab.

A slab is just an NFC tag that SLAB has learned to recognise. Almost any tag works, and there is a good chance one is already in your pocket.

Try what you own first

Before buying anything, open SLAB and try pairing one of these. If your phone reads it, it works, and you are done.

  • A transit card. An Opal, Myki, Oyster or similar.
  • A hotel room key you forgot to hand back.
  • An office or gym access card.
  • A library card, if it is the tap kind.

One catch worth knowing. A card you use every day makes a poor slab, because it travels with you, and the whole point is leaving the slab behind. Treat a card as a way to test the app, then get something you can abandon in another room.

What to buy

Look for NTAG213 stickers. They are the cheapest tag that does the job, usually sold in packs of ten or more for the price of a coffee. NTAG215 and NTAG216 also work and hold more data you will never use.

Size
25mm round stickers are the easy default. Larger reads slightly more reliably, smaller hides better.
On-metal tags
If you want to stick the tag to something metal, buy the version sold as "anti-metal" or "on-metal". A plain sticker on metal often will not read at all.
What to avoid
Anything described only as "NFC compatible" without naming the chip. Also skip NTAG203 and Mifare Classic, which are older and less reliable with iPhones.

Where

These are plain searches rather than specific products, because stock turns over constantly. Any seller with a sensible rating will do; the chips are all the same.

Amazon AU Search NTAG213 stickers Usually $12 to $25 for a pack, fastest delivery.
eBay AU Search NTAG213 stickers Often cheapest, slower if shipped from overseas.
AliExpress Search NTAG213 stickers Cheapest per tag, two to four weeks.

Making it a slab

The tag is the boring part. What matters is what you stick it to and where that thing lives.

  • Pick an object with some weight to it. A coaster, a stone, a block of wood, a book you like.
  • Put it somewhere that costs you something to reach. Another room, a drawer downstairs, the car.
  • Pair a second tag and leave it at work, or with someone you trust, so you are never genuinely stuck.

SLAB gives you five emergency unlocks for life, so a lost slab is an inconvenience rather than a disaster. They never reset, on purpose.
